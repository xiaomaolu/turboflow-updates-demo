TurboFlow Raises $6 Million Seed Round Led by Pantera Capital

Susquehanna Crypto and Digital Currency Group also participated in the round, supporting TurboFlow as it expands product development, liquidity infrastructure, and access for everyday traders.

Published June 22, 2026 By TurboFlow Singapore

TurboFlow has closed a $6 million seed funding round led by Pantera Capital, with participation from Susquehanna Crypto and Digital Currency Group.

The capital will support the next phase of TurboFlow's development, including product expansion, stronger liquidity infrastructure, and continued growth across global markets.

Building market infrastructure for everyday traders

TurboFlow is an on-chain trading ecosystem at the intersection of prediction markets and perpetual contracts. The platform is designed to make advanced market participation simpler, more transparent, and more accessible to everyday users.

Pantera's investment thesis highlights the growing convergence between prediction markets and perpetual futures, along with the opportunity to bring professional market infrastructure to a broader audience across Asia.

Trading, made simple. Everyone gets a seat at TurboFlow.

What the funding supports

TurboFlow plans to use the funding across three priorities: expanding its product offering, strengthening the liquidity and execution infrastructure behind its markets, and accelerating responsible global user growth.

These priorities support a single objective: creating an open trading ecosystem where users can access prediction markets and perpetual contracts through a clear, consumer-focused experience.

The next phase for TurboFlow

The seed round gives TurboFlow additional resources to develop its technology, deepen market quality, and expand the ecosystem around traders, liquidity providers, market makers, and partners.

With Pantera Capital, Susquehanna Crypto, and Digital Currency Group supporting the company, TurboFlow will continue building toward a more accessible and participatory future for on-chain markets.
